> About Aotuo Technology<br /><br />Aotuo Technology Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise specializing in the R&D, manufacturing, and service of drilling equipment. We are committed to providing high-performance, highly reliable professional mechanical equipment and solutions for global mineral resource exploration, hydrogeological construction, and infrastructure engineering. <br /><br />Core Business & Products<br />The company focuses on three major fields: well drilling, geological exploration, and engineering drilling. Our product line includes mechanical top-drive multifunctional drilling rigs, crawler-mounted well drilling rigs, wireline core drilling rigs, exploration sampling reverse circulation drilling rigs, truck-mounted drilling rigs, DTH drilling rigs, and submerged drilling blasting rigs, among other full-range equipment. Our equipment is characterized by high drilling efficiency, low construction costs, reliable operation, and ease of use, making it widely applicable to dozens of key industries such as mining, energy, water conservancy, transportation, and municipal engineering. <br /><br />Our Unique Value<br /><br />Customized R&D: We believe no single solution can solve all problems. Therefore, we offer in-depth "private customization" services, tailoring the most suitable drilling solutions based on your specific working conditions and needs. <br /><br />Global Service Network: Based in East China and serving nationwide, we also support global clients through our three operational centers in Wuxi, Chengdu, and Xinjiang. Our equipment has been exported to Australia, Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia, and Southeast Asia, with quality proven across diverse global working conditions. <br /><br />Worry-Free Full-Cycle Service: From professional transportation and on-site delivery to comprehensive operational training and technical support, we provide end-to-end services throughout the equipment lifecycle, ensuring you "buy with confidence and use with ease." <br /><br />Aotuo Technology adheres to the principle of "Quality First, Service Supreme," committed to creating tangible value for customers with reliable equipment. We look forward to collaborating with you to explore the infinite possibilities beneath the Earth.
